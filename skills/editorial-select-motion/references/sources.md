# Sources

Source: https://x.com/i/status/2105327069190344705, the Ideogram 4.5 launch film, pulled 2026-10-01 with `toolkit/ingest.sh` at 1920x1080, 24 fps, 47.38 s, sha256 621816df5367aed995c63803e7733c8053c92a7828b6794b18d5dffa6dfa4184. This file is the only place in the skill that names the source or its content. The film was the material the hand was measured from and the test the replica had to pass; nothing of its words, product, people, brand or photographs lives anywhere else in the skill.

## Where the source material lives

The full video and the dense frame strips and spectrograms the moves were read from are in `~/motion-expert/sources/editorial-select/` (`source.mp4`, `frames/`), committed in the repo but outside the skill. The source's measured numbers stay in the skill as `fingerprint.json` and `imagery.json`, which hold measurements only. The replica layer, everything a block needs to reproduce its source shot that belongs to the source rather than the hand (its words, traced glyphs, plate paths, sampled colours, per-shot calibration and the replica-only faces), is in `~/motion-expert/work/replica/presets/`: one `<block>.json` per block keyed by preset name and fed into a replica plan as `props.replica` by `apply.py`, the faces in `fonts/` served by the overlay style directory `style/` (`make_style.py`), and the traced glyph in `glyphs/`. The beat file the timing was checked against is `~/motion-expert/work/ideogram/music-beats.json`, and the beat-by-beat reading of the film is `~/motion-expert/work/ideogram/beats.json`.

## Never in prompts or pieces

The product name (Ideogram, ideogram.ai), the fictional brand on the boards (Velora), every on-screen line of the film (the six genre words on the title cards, the lockup line, the s3 statement line, the four imperatives, the interjection, the escalation words, the end-card sentence and its small line) and the people in its photographs. `toolkit/imagery.py` refuses prompt words listed as bullets under Never in `references/imagery.md`; that section stays free of source words by design, so check a prompt against this paragraph before generating.

## Gaps

Curves shorter than four frames (the title cards' handle streaks, the tile pops, the font cycle) could not be fitted under compression and the 16 fps cadence, so they are rebuilt from per-frame measurements in the blocks' replica presets and described by eye in `references/moves.md`. The pull-back's fitted curve (fingerprint move 71) spans two missed cuts, so the camera-rig preset refits it on frames 958 to 992 alone. Designed sound effects and the music share one stem that Demucs cannot separate, so the binding rules in `references/sound.md` are read from a spectrogram and rank events against each other rather than giving absolute levels, and the track itself is not identified. The haze in the photographs' highlights is described in words only, and the grain measured is what survived the encode (`references/imagery.md`). The two lighter-weight lines (near Exposure 0) cannot be thinned in CSS, so new work sets everything at the main weight.

The serif is 205TF Exposure (Federico Parra Barrios, 2022), set at different points on its exposure axis per scene. The match comes from 205TF's own specimen PDF, whose embedded subsets of every exposure step were rendered glyph by glyph over the film's own letter positions and scored by the house SSIM (960w, 1.0 prefilter, Wang window) in the word area: Exposure -20 scores 0.980 on "will never be the same" where the best open serif reaches 0.936, Exposure +10 scores 0.993 on "Try it now at ideogram.ai" against 0.953, and the one full subset in the specimen, Exposure 0, averages 0.934 over six paper lines, above every open face even on lines where its weight is wrong. Measured against Exposure 0, almost every line in the film sits about 9.5 units heavier per edge, which is where -20 falls, while "Big" and the end-card URL sit at 0 to +10. The speckled edges are not in the outlines, which are smooth in the specimen, so they come from the edit's grain and print treatment. Exposure is commercial, so nothing here ships it. Of 27 open serifs fitted the same way across their weight, optical size and softness axes, the best means over six lines are Source Serif 4 0.909 (opsz 17, wght 550), Crimson Pro 0.902, Merriweather 0.891, Literata 0.887 and Petrona 0.886 (the older 0.61 to 0.64 figure was raw full-resolution SSIM on the s1 step frames, a different measure). The style therefore uses its own face, EditorialSelect Serif in assets/fonts, with 36 glyphs traced from the film at the -20 weight and 65 filled from Source Serif 4 at matched stems and sidebearings (coverage in EditorialSelect-Serif-coverage.md). Set at the film's measured glyph positions it scores 0.991 on "Launch it", 0.979 on "Introducing Ideogram 4.5", 0.973 on "Model weights are coming soon" and 0.977 on "will never be the same"; set by word, with its own advances, kerning and each line's measured tracking, it scores 0.987, 0.960, 0.952 and 0.931, the last because the s1 sentence was set with tracking the per-line term does not fully absorb. Letters the film never shows (f j q x z, fourteen capitals, most figures and the punctuation) are Source Serif 4 shapes, so they read as the same family but not as Exposure.
