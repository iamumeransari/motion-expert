# Extraction

The source file is the ground truth, so the first job is getting the best copy that exists. `toolkit/ingest.sh <url-or-file> <slug>` asks yt-dlp for the highest video and audio streams merged to mp4 and writes `work/<slug>/source.mp4` with a `probe.json` recording duration, frame rate, size, codecs and a sha256. X serves full quality without login; LinkedIn often needs browser cookies, and when even that fails the script reports the post as gated and Umer drops the file into `inbox/`. A screen recording of a feed is a last resort that costs frame timing, and its use is recorded as a gap.

Measure the whole video, then look at what the measurement points to. `fingerprint.py` runs cut detection, the optical-flow energy curve, move detection with bezier fits, the look measures and the delivery measures, and writes one deterministic `fingerprint.json` plus tolerance bands. Cut detection separates hard cuts from dissolves and wipes, because motion-graphics transitions over-fire a naive frame difference; when the cut count looks wrong against the contact sheet, tune the threshold for that source and note it in `sources.md` rather than accepting the number.

Read the dense strips before writing any move down. A strip shows the move at twice the source frame rate, so overshoot, settle, stagger order and motion blur are visible as discrete frames. For each move write the timecode, the element, the property, the duration in frames and seconds, the fitted bezier with its fit error, and what the eye sees that the numbers miss, such as a one-frame flash, a blur trail or a squash on landing. Fits with high error stay in the inventory flagged for eye-checking, and a fit on a move shorter than about four frames at the source rate is a gap, because compression and frame quantisation leave too few samples to separate curve families.

Type is identified from the frames. When the face is recognisable, name it and find the real file; when it is not, measure x-height, cap height and set width from a large sample and pick the closest available face by those ratios, recording the candidates and the choice under Gaps. Colours come from the palette measure on clean frames, never from frames mid-transition or under motion blur.

The full source is copied into the style at `assets/source/source.mp4` and committed; files over 90MB go through Git LFS. Contact sheets and the strips for every signature move are kept in `assets/frames/` as the visual reference later agents compare against.
